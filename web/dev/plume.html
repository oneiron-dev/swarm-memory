Plume

A pigment study for the space behind a page.

Pigment, air, and time

What settles
stays.

A page can hold more than its words. Here, small grains of colour move through the space behind the text. Some rise on a current. Others fall, coming to rest where the last ones landed.

Change the ink and the air takes on a new colour. The ground remembers the old one. In time, these small arrivals become layers: a record of what passed through.

The words belong to the page. The canvas draws only grains.

Pigment controls

Waiting for frames.

Changes ease over 1.5 seconds. Old layers keep their ink. Measurement uses a 1440 × 900 CSS-pixel canvas, with 60 warm-up frames and 240 measured frames. CPU time covers simulation and canvas calls, not GPU presentation. The target is under 4 ms at DPR 2 on Apple M1.
